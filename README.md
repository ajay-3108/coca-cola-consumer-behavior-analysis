# 🥤 Coca-Cola Consumer Behavior & Purchase Intent Analysis

> A market research project focused on understanding the factors that influence consumer perceptions, preferences, and purchase intentions toward Coca-Cola carbonated beverages.

---

## 📸 Project Preview

### Data Adequacy Testing
<img width="1031" height="553" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f6a0ee70-da68-465b-8452-9fbf6ab11866" />

This study investigates consumer behavior related to Coca-Cola products using survey-based research and statistical analysis techniques.

The project aims to identify the key factors influencing customer preferences and evaluate the likelihood of consumers purchasing Coca-Cola products within the next 30 days.

---

## 🎯 Research Objectives

### Objective 1
Identify the factors influencing consumer perceptions and preferences regarding Coca-Cola carbonated drinks.

### Objective 2
Analyze the likelihood of consumers purchasing Coca-Cola products in the next 30 days.

---

| Metric | Value |

| Sampling Method | Convenience Sampling |

| Analysis Tool | IBM SPSS | Python |
| Research Type | Quantitative Research |

## 🔍 Data Adequacy Testing

Before performing Factor Analysis, data adequacy was tested.

### KMO Test

✅ KMO Value = **0.693**

### Bartlett's Test of Sphericity

✅ p-value < **0.001**

### Interpretation

The dataset satisfies the conditions required for Factor Analysis and is statistically suitable for dimensionality reduction.

## 🧠 Statistical Techniques Applied

### 1️⃣ Exploratory Factor Analysis (EFA)

Used to identify underlying dimensions influencing consumer behavior.

### 2️⃣ Principal Component Analysis (PCA)

Applied to reduce variables into meaningful factors.

### 3️⃣ Equamax Rotation

Used to improve interpretability of extracted factors.

### 4️⃣ Multinomial Logistic Regression

Used to predict purchase likelihood based on extracted factors.

## 🔑 Key Findings

### 🏪 Product Availability

Most influential factor affecting consumers who are highly likely to purchase Coca-Cola.

**Odds Ratio: 2.04 (+104%)**

---

### 📦 Packaging

Packaging significantly impacts purchase intention.

**Odds Ratio: 1.52 (+52%)**

---

### 🎯 Brand Promotion

Discounts and promotional campaigns positively influence buying behavior.

**Odds Ratio: 1.37 (+37%)**

---

### 📺 Advertisement & Lifestyle

Consumers show higher purchase intent when advertisements align with their lifestyle and values.

---

### 😋 Taste Preference

Taste consistency remains an important driver of brand loyalty.
